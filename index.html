Introduction

Adam Geggie-Bowker

Product Designer

Product designer based in Victoria, BC. I design connected hardware products from concept through production — bridging industrial design, firmware, and PCB layout into cohesive user experiences.

Co-founder of Remindr, where I lead hardware design and product development for smart IoT devices. My background in electrical engineering informs a systems-level approach to product design — every interface decision is grounded in what the hardware can deliver.

From custom PCB design and 3D-printed enclosures to captive portal UIs and WebGL marketing sites, I work across the full stack of what it takes to bring a physical product to life. I care about design that bridges the gap between digital and physical.
