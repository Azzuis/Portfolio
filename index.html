I co-founded Remindr — a smart product startup building ambient weather display devices. I've gone from schematic to manufactured PCB, 3D-printed enclosure, and shipping firmware. Previously I completed a naval architecture co-op designing propulsion systems for 110ft luxury yachts.

What it is

Rain Remindr is a physical ambient weather display device — an illuminated umbrella stand that glows different colours based on live weather forecasts. No screen, no app — just light.

Tech Stack

  • ESP32-C3 microcontroller
  • WS2811 COB LED strip (40 LEDs)
  • FastLED rendering engine
  • Open-Meteo weather API
  • WiFi manager + captive portal
  • OTA firmware updates
  • Custom PCB (manufactured)

My Role

Full-stack hardware: schematic design, PCB layout, firmware in C++ (PlatformIO), 3D-printed enclosure (PLA-CF), and product concept. Solo founder.
