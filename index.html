Project 01 — Consumer IoT

Rain
Remindr

A weather-reactive ambient light that glows when rain is forecast for your commute. ESP32-C3, Open-Meteo API, WS2811 LED ring, captive portal setup flow. First shipping product from Remindr.

A

Electrical

Schematic · Layout · Assembly
Stage 01

ECAD

Schematic and PCB layout work in KiCad. Designed around an ESP32-C3 module driving a WS2811 LED strip, with USB-C input stepped down by an AP63203WU buck converter. Focused on a compact two-layer board suitable for low-volume JLCPCB runs.

Stage 02

Breadboarding

Early validation of the LED driver, power supply, and Wi-Fi behaviour before committing to a PCB. Used a dev board and a short WS2811 strip to prove out the timing-critical bit-banged protocol and the captive portal join flow.

Protoboard

Hand-soldered perfboard version bridging the breadboard prototype and the first PCB. Useful for burn-in testing and checking mechanical fit inside the enclosure mock-up before paying for fabrication.

C

Programming

Firmware · API · UI
Stage 01

Firmware — v1.2.0

ESP32-C3 Arduino firmware polling Open-Meteo for rain probability on a configurable schedule and translating the forecast into colour and animation on a WS2811 strip. Sensitivity threshold, colour presets, and strip length are all user-tuneable.

main loop
Forecast → colour
Stage 02

Captive Portal UI

Browser-based setup flow served directly from the ESP32. Wi-Fi onboarding, location search, sensitivity slider, colour picker, and a strip-length finder that walks the user through calibrating their specific install.

Project 02 — Consumer IoT

Remindr
Pucks

Small, magnetic, battery-powered pucks that sit around your home and glow with ambient notifications from a central dock. nRF52832 BLE, SK6812MINI-E LED, LiPo with magnetic charging contacts.

A

Electrical

BLE · Power · Charging
Stage 01

ECAD

KiCad schematic around the nRF52832 with an MCP73831 LiPo charger, a single SK6812MINI-E addressable LED, and exposed pogo pads for magnetic charging. Careful attention to BLE antenna keepout and charger routing.
